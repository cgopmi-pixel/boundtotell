About us

Life stories, experiences, and family legacies should be preserved with care and cherished by generations of loved ones.

At Bound to Tell, we transform treasured memories into beautifully published books.

Memories are gathered through private, one-on-one interviews, with gentle guidance to recall details, stories, and anecdotes. Our authors organize the material into a clear, cohesive narrative and craft each volume in an engaging style suited to the story at hand. Design, layout, and graphics are handled with care and precision, culminating in a unique, published memoir.

Our mission is to give families a lasting legacy: a deep familiarity with their loved one's life story, and the ability to pass it on.
