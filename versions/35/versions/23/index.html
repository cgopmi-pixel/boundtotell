About us

At Bound to Tell, we help individuals and families turn treasured memories into a beautiful book. The stories of one's life and family deserve to be preserved with care so they can be cherished for generations.

Through personal, guided interviews, we gather those memories and shape them into a clear, engaging, and multi-chapter narrative. We bring together writing, editing, photo placement, and thoughtful design to create a memoir that will do its subject proud.

Our mission is to give families a lasting legacy: a deep familiarity with their loved one's life story, and the ability to pass it on.

Frequently asked questions

How does the process work?

We begin with a free initial consultation to discuss the story you’d like to preserve, the scope of the book, package options, estimated costs, and the expected timeline.

Through guided interviews, we gather the memories and shape them into a clear, engaging, multi-chapter narrative. We then bring together editing, photo placement, and interior and cover design. Finally, your family reviews the manuscript before the finished book is prepared for printing.

Does my parent need to prepare anything before the interviews?

No special preparation is needed. The interviewer will guide the conversation with questions that help bring out memories, experiences, and meaningful details. Your parent can simply share their story in their own words.

Can other family members contribute stories?

Yes. The number of interviewees included depends on the package you choose. Additional family members can be included through an upgrade, which we can discuss during the initial consult.

How do we choose the right package?

Consider how much of the story you’d like to preserve and how much detail you’d like the book to contain. Some families want to focus on selected memories, while others want a fuller life story or a broader family history. During the initial consult, we’ll help you choose the scope that fits your goals and budget.

Will we be able to review the manuscript before printing?

Yes. You’ll have the opportunity to review the manuscript and request changes through the revision rounds included in your package or upgrade.

One appointed family member will coordinate the review, gather everyone’s comments, and communicate to us feedback for each round. This helps keep the process organized and the family’s instructions consistent.

How long does a memoir project usually take?

The timeline depends on the scope of the project, including the interviews, writing, design, and family review. We’ll discuss the expected timeframe during your initial consult.

Can we order additional printed copies?

Yes. Additional copies are available by separate quote, based on the quantity and printing options you choose.
